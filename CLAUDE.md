# Collaborator instructions

See CONTRIBUTING.md for build and test commands. Preserve the scoped,
three-valued constraint model and the distinction between proposals and
host-reported executions. Keep policy changes separate from implementation
corrections. Scientific thresholds require maintainer review.

Report reproducible findings with expected and observed results. Attribute
prior work and maintain the claim boundaries in docs/design_lineage.md,
docs/validation.md, and SECURITY.md. Formal proofs cover their definitions;
they do not establish arbitrary external execution or sensor correctness.

Keep private research archives, credentials, local deployment state, and raw
conversations out of this repository.
